We are interested in understanding the molecular mechanisms underlying neurodegenerative diseases, particularly motor neuron diseases and Parkinson’s disease.

We integrate protein engineering with biochemical, biophysical, and cell biology approaches to investigate protein misfolding and the pathobiology of aberrant aggregation, develop human-relevant cellular disease models, translate laboratory findings into innovative tools, and develop potential therapeutic strategies.
